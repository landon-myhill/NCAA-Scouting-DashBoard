Why "The Boykins"

From stray to standard

Around 1905, a small brown spaniel — a stray nobody claimed — followed a banker home from church in Spartanburg, South Carolina. He wasn't big, wasn't pedigreed, and wasn't on anyone's list. The banker passed him to his hunting partner, Whit Boykin, who saw what the bloodlines couldn't show: the dog could hunt. That stray — "Dumpy" — became the foundation of the Boykin Spaniel, a champion bird-hunting breed and the official state dog of South Carolina.

That's the whole idea of this product. Draft pedigree is a mock ranking; talent is what shows up when you actually measure the work. We grade every eligible prospect on what he does — scoring, playmaking, rim pressure, defense — against the draft classes that came before him and the NBA careers they really produced. Sometimes the numbers point at the consensus #1. Sometimes they point at the stray.

Like any good bird dog, the model is judged only on whether it finds the bird: every ranking here is validated on draft classes it never saw, and it ships with its misses documented next to its hits.
